# H-001 — Owners will list their leftovers if it takes under a minute

Grew from: VISION, S-001

## Belief
We believe that a cafe owner lists the evening's leftovers when doing so takes less than a minute.

## Test
Five owners use the prototype for one week, at closing.

## Metric
The share of evenings on which an owner listed something.

## Threshold
At least three evenings out of five, for at least three of the five owners.

## Result
R-001

- S-001 L12 "it has to take a minute"
