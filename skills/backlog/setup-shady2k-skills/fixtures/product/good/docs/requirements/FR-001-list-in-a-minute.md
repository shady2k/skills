# FR-001 — A cafe lists its leftovers in under a minute

Serves: UC-001, US-001
Verified by: demonstration

## Requirement
An owner who has their menu set up lists tonight's leftovers, with portions and price, in under a minute and in at most four taps.

## Rationale
An owner at closing has no time to spare (H-001).

## Sources
- S-001 L12 "I have no time to post anything, it has to take a minute"
