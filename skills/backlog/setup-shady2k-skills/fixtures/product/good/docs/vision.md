# Leftovers: small cafes sell what is left at closing

## Audience
Owners of small cafes with fewer than twenty seats, who throw away food every evening.

## Problem
Unsold portions are thrown away at closing; the owner loses their cost and dislikes the waste (S-001).

## Outcome
A cafe lists what is left before closing, and people nearby buy it at a discount.

## Exclusions
Delivery, chains with their own apps, payment inside the product in the first version.
